Prompt Engineering

Learning how to communicate effectively with Artificial Intelligence.

What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt Engineering is the process of writing clear and specific instructions (prompts) to guide an Artificial Intelligence tool and obtain useful results.

How to Write a Good Prompt

🎯

Be Specific

Explain exactly what you need instead of using vague instructions.

📝

Give Context

Provide useful information about the task and its purpose.

💡

Define the Format

Specify whether you want a table, explanation, summary or example.

Example of a Prompt

My Prompt

"Explain the basics of cybersecurity in simple English. Use short paragraphs and give three practical examples."

Why is it effective?

It specifies the subject, language, difficulty level and expected format.

Responsible Use of AI

AI can help us learn, understand and improve our work. However, its answers must always be checked and verified. We should never share passwords, private information or sensitive documents with AI tools.

What I Learned

I learned that a good prompt should be clear, specific and well-organized. I also understood the importance of checking AI-generated information and using AI responsibly.
