CHAPTER 01

Introduction to Cybersecurity

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, systems and data against digital attacks.

Why is it important?

It helps protect personal information, prevent unauthorized access and maintain the confidentiality of digital data.

UNDERSTANDING THREATS

Common Cyber Threats

Virus

Malicious software that attaches itself to files or programs and spreads when executed.

Phishing

Fraudulent messages designed to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

Ransomware

Malware that blocks access to files and demands payment.

Spyware

Software that secretly collects information about users.

STAY PROTECTED

Security Best Practices

♡ Use strong and unique passwords.

♡ Enable two-factor authentication (2FA).

♡ Keep your software updated.

♡ Avoid suspicious links and attachments.

♡ Be careful when using public Wi-Fi.

♡ Never share passwords or sensitive information.
